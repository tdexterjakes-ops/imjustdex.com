False positives caught before filing

Each of these was produced confidently by an automated check this run and discarded after being chased down. All four are now recorded in audit/QA-OS.md under a new MEASUREMENT ANTIPATTERNS section so future runs stop rediscovering them.

Phantom #1 — "the mode toggle is broken on article pages"

A programmatic element.click() flips html.dark-mode, the --bg/--ink tokens and the dxmode cookie, but leaves body's used background and color stale in getComputedStyle — for over a second, past a forced reflow. It read as a hard mode-toggle regression. Setting body.style.transition='none' unstuck it instantly, which identified the artifact; a real pointer click plus a screenshot then showed the toggle repainting perfectly. Verdict: no defect.

Phantom #2 — "no focus rings anywhere on the site"

Chrome does not apply :focus-visible to programmatic element.focus(), so all 34 interactive elements reported outline: none. Real Tab presses show the designed ring: solid 2px rgb(204,0,0). Verdict: no defect.

Phantom #3 — "white-on-cream text on every image plate"

Image plates paint their surface with ::before (an opaque img-concrete/img-noise/img-lines gradient) plus an ::after black gradient. Walking background-color up the tree sees straight through both to the page ground and reports ~1.06:1 for every title, deck and lane chip. The CSS is explicit that these plates are light-on-dark by construction, .plate-lane carries its own rgba(0,0,0,.72) chip, and .meta-rail carries an opaque cream rail. Verdict: no defect.

Dismissed again — the homepage .dot separator at 4.25–4.30:1

Real measurement, but not a defect: the · between date and read-time is aria-hidden="true" pure decoration, and both of its siblings pass on their own. This was already reasoned through in the F89 fix comment (css/notfound.css:64-69), which fixed the /404 divider precisely because that one is not aria-hidden. Now on the known-intentional list so it stops resurfacing.
