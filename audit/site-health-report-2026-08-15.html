The headline

Four of today's five fixes were one bug wearing different clothes

--body-muted is an alpha token — rgba(243,240,232,.52) dark, rgba(5,5,5,.56) light — deliberately tuned to land just over the 4.5:1 AA floor on its own. Four separate rules stacked a second opacity multiplier on top of it (or on top of an already-correct solid token). Compositing twice breaks the tuning every time.

Worth having as a standing rule: if a colour comes from a muted token, do not re-mute it. A grep for opacity in the same block as var(--body-muted) would have caught all four in one pass.
